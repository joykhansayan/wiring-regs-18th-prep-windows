Email dmjks120@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can. Please include your Windows version and a short description of the issue.

Restore purchases

If you already subscribed or bought Lifetime Access, open the paywall or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Visit account.microsoft.com/services, sign in, and choose 18th Edition Wiring Regs Prep under Services & subscriptions.

Mock exams

Mock exams have 60 questions in 2 hours and are scored to a pass mark of about 60% (36 of 60), like the open-book City & Guilds 2382-26 exam. Keep your own copy of BS 7671 beside you while you practise.

Keyboard shortcuts

In a quiz: A–H or 1–8 to answer, Enter for next, ← for the previous question, Esc to leave. In flashcards: Space to flip, 1 review again, 2 got it.
